# Single image post (and the 2-slide fast post)

One frame that has to work alone in the feed, at 1080×1350. Use it when one fact, one
chart, or one quote is the whole story; a carousel would only pad it.

## Kinds

| Kind | Frame | When | In the data |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One chart** | title as a finding, sorted horizontal bars from zero, one bar highlighted, source and n at the bottom | a survey or dataset with one clear finding | Tirto #CukupTau, Katadata Databoks (chart is the cover) |
| **Fast news** | photo + headline box with the date and the local price; optional second slide with the full price list | a launch, a price, an availability date in the reader's country | teknologi_id's iPhone pre-order post, the account's best in the sample |
| **Quote card** | the quote large in the display italic, name, role, date, small portrait or none | a named person said the thing in a way worth keeping | Narasi quote slides |
| **Artefact** | the real screenshot in colour, one highlighted line, a headline that says what it proves | the evidence is the story (a commit, a changelog, a price page) | creator posts on X |
| **Announcement** | product name + one fact + one visual | the brand's own launch or event | |

## Writing it

- The headline carries the finding as a sentence with a verb: "Separuh siswa mengaku AI
  mengerjakan hampir separuh tugasnya." Not a topic label.
- Numbers exact, with the base: "51% dari 1.294 responden". Source and n on the frame,
  not only in the caption.
- One frame, one claim. If you need a second claim, it is a carousel.
- The caption tells the rest (`copy-playbook.md` section 6), and may end with one real
  question.

## Chart rules

Bars start at zero; sort them; highlight the one the headline is about; label values
directly on the bars; no 3D, no pie with more than three slices; source line with the
survey's owner, date, and n. Recipe in `design-system.md`.

## Checklist

- [ ] Readable at thumbnail size: headline ≤ 10 words, ≥ 64 px.
- [ ] Source and n on the frame.
- [ ] Nothing important in the outer 90 px (3:4 grid crop).
- [ ] Alt text written.
